## 🚨 合规提醒

1. **CAN-SPAM Act**: Include physical address in footer, clear unsubscribe link
2. **GDPR**: For EU contacts, ensure legitimate interest basis for processing
3. **Anti-Spam Best Practices**: 
   - Never buy email lists — use Hunter.io or Apollo.io for fresh leads
   - Keep subject lines <50 characters
   - Avoid all-caps, excessive punctuation (!!!), and trigger words ("FREE", "ACT NOW")
   - Personalize each email (use company name + specific GEO insight)
