### Section 1: The Problem (Why Traditional SEO Isn't Enough Anymore)
- 展示Notion GEO Audit数据截图（Citation Rate 78%, Position 2.3, GEO Health Score 74/B）
- 引用SEM Rush实验核心数据：Owned Content贡献仅14%，Earned Placement占86%
- 关键洞察："SEO→GEO强相关但不够；generative engine通常只给3-5个引用位（winner-takes-all格局）"

### Section 2: What Actually Works (3 Data-Backed Strategies)

**Strategy A: Comparative Content Has a 12.25× Multiplier**
- 实验数据：加入"vs"竞品对比页后，单关键词引用量从4→49
- 具体做法：为每个核心产品创建3-5个comparison landing pages
- 模板提示词："[Your Product] vs [Competitor]: A 2026 Feature-by-Feature Comparison"

**Strategy B: Listicle Placement > All Owned Content Combined**
- SEM Rush数据：Indeed SEO Listicle单篇驱动190次提及 > 其他所有来源总和
- Brett Farmiloe(HARO创始人)原话："Use AI to do research, not to send pitches"
- 实操清单：每日15分钟HARO检查 + Connectively订阅过滤高DA dofollow机会

**Strategy C: Schema Markup is Table Stakes**
- Notion审计发现：40%的GEO Health Score缺口来自技术层面（缺失JSON-LD）
- 修复后Citation Rate从42%→78%
- 七种必装Schema：FAQPage/Product/Article/HowTo/Organization/BreadcrumbList/Review
- 免费工具：Google Rich Results Test + JSON-LD Validator
